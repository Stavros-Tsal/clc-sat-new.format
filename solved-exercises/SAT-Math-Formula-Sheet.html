01Circles

Equation of a circle: \((x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2\)

Center: \((h, k)\)    Radius: \(r\)

Circumference: \(C = 2\pi r\)

Area: \(A = \pi r^2\)

Radians ↔ Degrees

\(\dfrac{\pi}{180} = 1^\circ\)

\(\dfrac{180}{\pi} = 1 \text{ radian}\)

Arc Length: \(s = r\theta\)

Central Angle

Angle formed at the center by two radii.

Inscribed Angle

Angle formed on the circle; it's half of the central angle.
central \(= \dfrac{1}{2}\,\)inscribed

04Triangles

Similar Triangles

Ratio of sides: \(\dfrac{a_1}{a_2} = \dfrac{b_1}{b_2} = \dfrac{c_1}{c_2}\)

Ratio of areas: \(\left(\dfrac{a_1}{a_2}\right)^2 = \dfrac{\text{Area}_1}{\text{Area}_2}\)

Ratio of volumes (3D): \(\left(\dfrac{a_1}{a_2}\right)^3 = \dfrac{\text{Vol}_1}{\text{Vol}_2}\)

Congruent Triangles → Same angles and lengths

Similar Triangles → Same angles only and common ratio on their lengths

Κριτηρια Ισοτητας Τριγωνων: Π-Π-Π, Π-Γ-Π (περιεχομενη γωνια), Γ-Π-Γ (προσπιπτουσες γωνιες στην πλευρα). Κριτηριο Ομοιων Τριγωνων: 2 ισες γωνιες αρκουν.

Special Triangles

\(45^\circ\text{–}45^\circ\text{–}90^\circ \rightarrow\) sides: \(x,\ x,\ x\sqrt{2}\)

\(30^\circ\text{–}60^\circ\text{–}90^\circ \rightarrow\) sides: \(x,\ x\sqrt{3},\ 2x\)

Isosceles:

Two sides are equal → base angles are equal.

Equilateral:

All sides are equal, all angles = 60°.

08Linear Graphs

Slope: \(\dfrac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}\)

Slope-Intercept Form: \(y = mx + b\)

Point-Slope Form: \(y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)\)

Distance Formula: \(d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}\)

Midpoint Formula: \((x, y) = \left(\dfrac{x_1 + x_2}{2},\ \dfrac{y_1 + y_2}{2}\right)\)

Horizontal Line: \(y = c,\ m = 0\)

Quadratic Formula: \(x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}\)

Perpendicular Lines

Slopes are opposite reciprocals.

\(3 = \dfrac{3}{1} \rightarrow \dfrac{1}{3} \rightarrow -\dfrac{1}{3}\)

Flip, then switch sign.

Reference

  • \(m\) = slope
  • \(b\) = y-intercept (constant)
  • \(d\) = distance
  • \(c\) = constant

09Exponents

Fundamental Laws

Product Rule: \(a^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n}\)

Quotient Rule: \(\dfrac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n},\ a \neq 0\)

Power of a Power: \(\left(a^m\right)^n = a^{mn}\)

Power of a Product: \((ab)^n = a^n b^n\)

Power of a Quotient: \(\left(\dfrac{a}{b}\right)^n = \dfrac{a^n}{b^n},\ b \neq 0\)

Negative Exponent: \(a^{-n} = \dfrac{1}{a^n},\ a \neq 0\)

Zero Exponent: \(a^0 = 1,\ a \neq 0\)

Identity Exponent: \(a^1 = a\)

Fractional Exponent: \(a^{\frac{m}{n}} = \sqrt[n]{a^m},\ a > 0\)

Root Rule: \(\sqrt[n]{a} = a^{\frac{1}{n}},\ a > 0\)

Even/Odd Power of −1: \((-1)^n = \begin{cases} 1, & \text{if } n \text{ is even} \\ -1, & \text{if } n \text{ is odd} \end{cases}\)

Rational Exponent: \(a^{\frac{m}{n}} = \left(\sqrt[n]{a}\right)^m,\ a > 0\)

Exponential Growth & Decay

\(f(t) = a(r)^t\)

  • \(a\): Initial value (y-intercept)
  • \(r\): Rate of change
  • \(t\): Time
Example: 200 bacteria double every 3 hours — when will the population reach 800?

\(f(t) = 200(2)^{\frac{t}{3}}\)

\(800 = 200(2)^{\frac{t}{3}}\)

\(4 = 2^{\frac{t}{3}}\)

\(\dfrac{t}{3} = 2 \quad\Rightarrow\quad t = 6\)

Answer: Population will reach 800 after 6 hours.

10Quadratic Graphs

Standard Form: \(y = ax^2 + bx + c\)

  • a: The greater \(a\) is, the narrower the graph. If \(a\) is positive, the graph opens upward (U-shape); if negative, it opens downward (∩-shape).
  • b: Represents the slope of the parabola at the y-intercept, or when \(x = 0\).
  • c: The y-value of the graph at the y-intercept.

Vertex Form: \(y = a(x - h)^2 + k\)

  • h: The x-value of the vertex.
  • k: The y-value of the vertex.

Quadratic Formula: \(x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}\)

Discriminant: \(b^2 - 4ac\)

  • \(> 0\) → there are 2 solutions
  • \(= 0\) → there is 1 solution
  • \(< 0\) → there are no solutions
